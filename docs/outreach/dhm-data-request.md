# DHM data request (email draft)

To: DHM data section (get address from https://www.dhm.gov.np/ contact page). Verify station index numbers against DHM station pages before sending. Consider a near-copy to NEA Generation Directorate for Kulekhani reservoir data (NEA operates the plant).

**Subject:** Request for Historical Hydrological and Meteorological Data – Educational Research on Nepal Electricity Demand Forecasting

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request historical hydrological and meteorological data from the Department of Hydrology and Meteorology for an educational research project.

The project studies Nepal's electricity demand and supply, with a focus on the winter dry season. In winter, river flows drop, run-of-river generation falls, and Nepal relies more on imports from India. I am building a forecasting model that links river flow, reservoir conditions and weather to national demand and import needs, using public data from the Nepal Electricity Authority. The work is non-commercial and for learning only. DHM will be cited as the data source in all outputs, and the raw data will not be redistributed.

**Requested stations (priority order):**

| # | Station / Site | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kulekhani reservoir and Kulekhani/Bagmati-area stations | Nepal's only storage reservoir; key to winter peak supply |
| 2 | Tamakoshi at Busti (Index 647) | Upper Tamakoshi (456 MW), the largest plant on the grid |
| 3 | Kali Gandaki at Setibeni (Index 420) | Kali Gandaki A (144 MW), the largest NEA run-of-river plant |
| 4 | Trishuli at Betrawati (Index 447) | Trishuli, Devighat, Upper Trishuli 3A and several IPP plants |
| 5 | Marsyangdi at Bimalnagar (Index 439.7) | Marsyangdi, Middle Marsyangdi and Upper Marsyangdi plants |

If possible, also: Sunkoshi at Pachuwarghat (630) and Koshi at Chatara (695).

**Data requested:**
1. Daily mean discharge (m³/s) for the stations above.
2. Daily water level / gauge height (m) for the same stations.
3. Kulekhani reservoir level, storage and inflow (daily), if DHM holds it.
4. Daily precipitation (mm) at meteorological stations in these catchments.
5. Daily maximum and minimum temperature (°C) for Kathmandu, Biratnagar, Simara/Birgunj, Bhairahawa, Nepalgunj and Dhangadhi.

**Period:** daily values from 2010 to the latest available date. If longer records are easy to share, data from 2000 onwards would help further.

**Format:** CSV or Excel is preferred, but any format DHM normally provides is fine.

I am happy to follow DHM's standard data request procedure, fill in any forms, and pay any applicable fee. Please let me know if a formal application or letter from my institution is needed. I would also be glad to share the final findings with DHM.

Thank you for your time and support.
